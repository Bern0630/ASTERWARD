**Primary thesis:** A US 10-year yield near 5.25% and weak Malaysian technology pressured Taiwan's reopening, but nearby Taiwan index futures had rebounded from 47,769 to about 48,166 by 9:45 a.m., returning to the threshold for moving from defensive to neutral. The key test is now **whether 48,155-48,170 is confirmed by a completed 30-minute candle and a successful retest**.

**Secondary signal 1:** The US decline did not become a panic, but higher yields, oil and the dollar kept financial conditions tight and left the discount-rate pressure on long-duration electronics unresolved.

**Secondary signal 2:** Foreign investors sold Taiwan cash equities and raised their Taiwan index futures net short to 77,031 contracts in the last session. Today's rebound improves the price signal, but current institutional and futures positions are not yet available, so the stance can rise only to conditional neutral.

The prior call correctly identified rates as the driver and captured the index direction and broad technology pressure. Nvidia's company-specific support, however, shows that today's Taiwan view cannot apply the US technology decline mechanically to every semiconductor stock. The baseline remains defensive, but actual Taiwan breadth and heavyweight performance must provide confirmation.

### Transmission Thesis

The US 10-year yield rose to about 5.25%, while WTI and Brent settled near USD 92.60 and USD 105.28. Energy inflation and funding costs are therefore tightening in the same direction, and Nasdaq underperformance transmits first into Taiwan's high-valuation semiconductor and AI supply chain. Weak Malaysian technology and mid-caps reinforce the regional risk-reduction signal. **Taiwan should therefore process valuation and hedging pressure first, rather than price an abrupt collapse in end demand.**

### Previous US Signals

The S&P 500 closed at 7,683.69, down 0.77%; the Dow ended at 51,481.51, down 0.67%; and the Nasdaq finished at 26,820.38, down 0.92%. The 10-year yield was about 5.251% and the dollar strengthened. Oil pared its intraday gains but still settled higher, so financial conditions did not genuinely ease. Nvidia rose 1.7% after expanding its repurchase authorization, making the US message "bearish indices and discount rates, but continued company-level differentiation," not a collapse in chip demand.

**What this means for Taiwan:** TSMC and large electronics heavyweights face catch-up pressure, but suppliers with direct catalysts or visible orders need not weaken uniformly. If semiconductors open lower and quickly narrow their losses, the session may shift from broad catch-up selling to stock selection.

### Previous Malaysia Signals

The FBM KLCI closed at 1,670.02, down 0.09%, but 748 shares declined against only 388 advancers and turnover fell to 3.11 billion shares. Inari fell to MYR 2.55, while Malaysian Pacific Industries and Kelington also recorded material losses. Support from financial heavyweights masked weak technology and mid-cap internals.

**What this means for Taiwan:** Malaysia does not directly determine Taiwan's direction, but it confirms that Asian investors reduced technology and mid-cap exposure first under high rates. The quality of electronics buying after Taiwan opens therefore matters more than whether the headline index briefly turns positive.

### Taiwan Fundamentals and Technicals

Taiwan's previous cash session closed at 48,024.60, down 0.28%, with the semiconductor index down 0.66%. There were 386 advancers and 546 decliners, as heavyweight semiconductors lagged selected electronic components. During the closure, nearby FTSE Taiwan futures closed at 4,258.00, down 0.33%, in line with weakness in US and Malaysian technology, though this remains only an offshore proxy.

Initial support is 47,876, followed by the ability to reclaim the 47,800 area quickly after any break. The 48,155-48,170 zone is the decision area for moving from defensive to neutral, while 48,647 remains overhead resistance. **A lower open followed by narrowing semiconductor losses and improving breadth would indicate controlled repricing. A break below 47,876 with deteriorating breadth would indicate risk reduction rather than a simple opening gap.**

Opening volume must distinguish absorption from distribution. If turnover expands after a gap lower but the index stops making new lows, holiday selling is probably being absorbed. If volume rises while index lows continue to fall and the OTC market weakens as well, pressure has spread from heavyweight catch-up selling into smaller stocks. The first pattern supports consolidation; only the second calls for an additional reduction in exposure.

The 9:45 a.m. continuous nearby Taiwan index futures chart showed a rebound from an intraday low of 47,769 to about 48,166, testing the five-day average near 48,174 and the 48,155-48,170 pivot. The latest 30- and 15-minute candles were unfinished, so an intrabar move above the pivot is not a confirmed breakout. **The rebound invalidates a persistent one-way catch-up decline, but it does not yet confirm a new upswing.** A completed 30-minute close above the pivot, a successful retest and a higher low on the 15-minute chart would put 48,647 and then 48,946 in view. A close back below the pivot followed by a failed recovery would expose 48,048 and then 47,769. These are nearby futures levels and are not interchangeable with the Taiex cash index.

TSMC ADRs gained about 0.50% in the previous US session, providing a relative-strength backdrop for the heavyweight. The ADR and Taiwan-listed shares are different instruments, however. TSMC cash trading must align with nearby futures, and memory shares must strengthen as a group, before either becomes confirmation. No entry level is assigned to individual stocks without same-time charts.

### Taiwan Positioning

The latest confirmed cash and derivatives data remains September 24 and must not be treated as current-session positioning. Foreign investors sold TWD 32.97 billion, investment trusts sold TWD 12.82 billion and dealers bought TWD 1.34 billion, leaving the three institutions with combined net selling of TWD 44.45 billion. Foreign investors held 9,675 long and 86,706 short Taiwan index futures contracts, a net short of 77,031. Options volume Put/Call stood at 121.11%, with the open-interest ratio at 85.33%.

Margin positions totaled 9.2797 million lots and TWD 615.10 billion; short selling stood at 202,000 lots, while securities-lending sales balances were 31.933 billion shares. **Foreign cash selling, a larger futures hedge and higher margin value together leave leveraged long exposure greater than the evidence of institutional support.** Genuine improvement today requires narrower foreign selling, no further increase in the futures net short, and price support. Current-session institutional, futures, options, margin and securities-lending data remains unavailable before the close and must be checked afterward.

### Taiwan Session Outlook

The early low reached 47,769 before nearby futures rebounded toward 48,166. The original lower-open stabilization path was therefore confirmed, but 48,155-48,170 is now the decision area. The baseline shifts from defensive to **conditional neutral** only if a completed 30-minute candle holds the pivot, a retest succeeds, and TSMC and breadth improve together. A move back below 48,155 followed by a failed recovery returns the market to consolidation, with 48,048 and 47,769 as successive risk thresholds.

**Suggested exposure:** Conditional neutral. Do not chase inside the 48,155-48,170 decision zone; require a completed 30-minute candle, a successful retest and improving breadth. Return to defensive below the pivot.

**Sector tilt:** First test whether TSMC and semiconductors maintain relative strength. Memory requires group confirmation. Keep stable-cash-flow and lower-valuation groups as the defensive allocation until price and volume confirm broader electronics leadership.

**Validity horizon:** The September 29 Taiwan regular session. Use only completed intraday candles, then score the call after the close with institutional, futures, options, margin and securities-lending data.

### Invalidation Conditions

Conditional neutral fails if a completed 30-minute candle cannot hold 48,155 and a rebound cannot recover it; 48,048 is the first downside check. A further break below 47,769 with weaker breadth restores a defensive stance. Conversely, a confirmed hold above 48,170, a successful retest and a break above 48,647 would upgrade the repair into a broader upswing, with 48,946 as the next threshold. Lower US yields would improve the backdrop but cannot replace Taiwan candle, breadth and positioning confirmation.
