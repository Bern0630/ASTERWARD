## What is changing?

AI infrastructure is shifting from a pure compute scarcity narrative into a broader capital cycle involving land, power, cooling, debt capacity and long-term offtake.

## Why now?

The next research question is no longer whether demand exists. It is whether financing, grid access and utilization can convert announced capacity into economic capacity.

## End demand

End demand should be separated into contracted demand, expected demand and speculative demand. Each carries different credit quality.

## Weakest link

The weakest link may be the transition from announced demand to financed, grid-connected and revenue-producing infrastructure.

## Next confirmation

The next confirmation point is whether announced capacity becomes power-secured and financing-secured without materially weaker economics.
